\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We turned negative transfer in meta-learning from an accuracy symptom into an explicit, post-hoc explanation. Adaptation gain $\Delta M$ answers \emph{whether} adaptation helped; \fama answers \emph{where}, extending Grad-CAM across the trajectory via an $\mathcal{O}(TP)$ adjoint recursion, needing no meta-training data. Two faithfulness protocols tie this causally to $S_i$ ($p<0.001$, Conv4 and ResNet-10 alike), and \fama recovers \emph{contextual bias} -- the backbone leaning on background when the object is weak.
